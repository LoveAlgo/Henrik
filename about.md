I look for beauty in people, in relationships, in the places we live and in the stories that shape what we dream of. Most of my work grows out of that interest, and each project looks at it from a different side.

## Background

I was born in Australia to Swedish parents and grew up in Geneva, surrounded by European standards of taste and refinement. One of my first summer jobs, at sixteen, was at Harry Winston: an early lesson in craftsmanship, elegance and restraint. Since then I have lived in nine countries and dozens of homes. Today I live in the south of France.

I hold an MBA from the University of St. Gallen, and I have studied urban and regional planning, architecture, sustainable living, and adult education. Professionally, I have worked in finance, entrepreneurship and research, and in designing compatibility systems for relationships and for place-based living.

## How I work

I am a researcher by nature. I have studied thousands of images, read hundreds of books, and explored psychology, aesthetics, self-development and teaching. I rarely stay inside one discipline. A chapter of a novel becomes an urban planning project. Research for a mentorship shapes a character. Years of moving between countries become an algorithm for choosing where to live.

In 2023 I wrote *Rethink Everything*, a manuscript that has not been published, but much of its content is now being adapted for Geneva 2061.

## What I’m looking for

The projects are well under way, but they have reached the point where my own time is the main limit. I’m looking for people who want to help take them further: research and technical partners for the compatibility algorithms, film financing and a publishing partner for Grooters, and funding for Geneva 2061, Healfi, and Hectare 1.
