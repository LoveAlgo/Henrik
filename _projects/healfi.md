Healfi looks at health as a whole life rather than the absence of illness. Its framework puts the person at the centre, surrounded by twelve areas that shape wellbeing: nutrition, exercise, mindset, time, fun, environment, feng shui, work, finances, helping others, other people, and a life partner.

## Where it stands

I built the original Healfi website about twenty years ago, and it is still online at healfi.me. The framework has held up well; the website needs updating.

## What comes next

Two new formats are planned: a card deck, to make the twelve areas easy to explore and discuss, and an e-course that walks through them one by one.

## How it connects

Several of Healfi’s areas link directly to my other projects. The life partner area connects to [Compalgo](/compalgo/), the environment area to [Placealgo](/placealgo/), and the holistic, inside-out approach is shared with [Learned Beauty](/learnedbeauty/).

## What would help

Funding, which buys the time to rebuild the website and create the card deck and the e-course.
