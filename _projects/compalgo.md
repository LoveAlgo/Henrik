Compalgo answers a simple question: how well do two people fit together? You enter two individuals, and the algorithm returns an assessment of their romantic compatibility.

## Why it matters

Choosing a life partner is one of the most consequential decisions anyone makes. Most dating tools are built to help people meet. Compalgo looks at what comes after meeting: whether two people are likely to suit each other over the long term.

## Where it stands

The first version of the algorithm works and is live on compalgo.com, alongside a page on the science behind it. The next step is more research, to refine the model and test it against real relationships.

## A family of compatibility algorithms

Compalgo is one of three related algorithms, each covering one of life’s big choices:

- **Compalgo**: who to share your life with
- **[Placealgo](/placealgo/)**: where to live
- **A career compatibility algorithm**: what work to do (planned)

They share an approach, so research and development on one strengthens the others.

## What would help

Research partners, for example in relationship psychology or data science, technical partners to develop the product further, and funding for the research.
