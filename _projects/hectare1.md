Creating a family homestead is one of the most complex projects a family can take on. Hectare 1 helps people plan and build one: a home on a hectare of land, designed to feed, house and sustain a family for generations.

## The idea

The site calls this kind of homestead a “Space of Love”. It differs from an ordinary house with a garden in several ways:

- **Built for generations**, not just for one lifetime.
- **Food first.** A vegetable garden and fruit trees feed the family, with extra to sell, alongside flowers for beauty and pollination.
- **Self-sufficient.** Energy and water come from the land itself, so the family can live off the grid, apart from the internet.
- **One hectare.** That is enough room to create a small ecosystem of its own, with space for creative solutions.
- **No lifelong mortgage.** The project is financed in ways that don’t tie the family to an unhealthy job for thirty years.
- **Built by the family**, with help from friends and professionals where needed, which keeps costs down and brings real satisfaction.
- **A setting for a good life**: calm, healthy and inspiring, a place where relationships, creative work and a livelihood can grow.

The concept draws on the ecovillage movement inspired by Vladimir Megré’s book series *The Ringing Cedars of Russia*, in which families each settle on about a hectare of land and together form a village.

## The structure

A homestead involves hundreds of decisions, from land and financing to roofs, compost, schooling and the running of a village. Hectare 1 gathers them all in one place. Its menu has eight main sections: Planning, Garden, House, Partner, Children, Livelihood, Village and Miscellaneous. Each one opens onto subcategories and then onto specific topics, such as Garden, then Landscaping, then Pond, or House, then Building, then Electricity, then Solar. Any topic can be found in two or three clicks.

## Where it stands

I built the site in 2016. The structure is complete, but most categories still need to be written. Like Geneva 2061 and Healfi, what the project needs most is time and funding to fill it with content.
